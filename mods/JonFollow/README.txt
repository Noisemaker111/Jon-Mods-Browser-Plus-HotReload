Jon's Follow (Beta)

Press Tab, right-click a party member within 20 m and choose Follow, then close your inventory. You walk the way a player does: you turn to face your route, walk when they walk and sprint (using stamina) when they sprint or pull ahead, step up ledges and route around walls and drops. Follow stays on until you move, jump, attack, use something, die or leave the party, or until your friend gets more than 45 m ahead. A friend who is down nearby is waited for. You can also choose Stop following with your inventory open. Follow works on foot and while driving your own bicycle, minibike, motorcycle or 4x4; vehicles brake at obstacles and drops. Follow does not enter vehicles or fly gyrocopters. Works with the standard party HUD or Jon's Party Portraits.

Installation
Close the game. Extract the ZIP and copy the JonFollow folder into %AppData%\7DaysToDie\Mods. Start the game with EAC disabled. For multiplayer, install the same version on the host and every player.

Beta
Experimental beta for 7 Days to Die V3.2.0 (b10). In-game behavior and multiplayer are unverified.
