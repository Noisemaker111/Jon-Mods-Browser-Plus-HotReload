Jon's Party Portraits (Beta)

Shows your party members on the left with a head-and-shoulders portrait, name, level, unspent skill points, health, stamina and an XP bar. XP still owed from a death penalty shows in red, and a fresh level-up is highlighted. Distance sits next to the direction arrow. Portraits update when their equipment changes. Works with Jon's Follow. Other mods that replace the party HUD may conflict.

Installation
Close the game. Extract the ZIP and copy the JonPartyPortraits folder into %AppData%\7DaysToDie\Mods. Start the game with EAC disabled. For multiplayer, install the same version on the host and every player.

Beta
Experimental beta for 7 Days to Die V3.2.0 (b10). In-game behavior and multiplayer are unverified.

Permissions
Re-uploading or including this mod in a public mod pack requires the author's permission.
