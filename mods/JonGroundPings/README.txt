Jon's Ground Pings (Beta)

Aim at a location during gameplay and middle-click to place three pulsing cyan ground arrows. The ping lasts six seconds and fades during the last two. A new ping replaces your previous one. Party members see your ping; outside a party, only you see it. Aiming at a wall places the marker on the ground below it. Pings are disabled while the map, inventory or another menu is open.

Installation
Close the game. Extract the ZIP and copy the JonGroundPings folder into %AppData%\7DaysToDie\Mods. Start the game with EAC disabled. For multiplayer, install the same version on the host and every player.

Beta
Experimental beta for 7 Days to Die V3.2.0 (b10). In-game behavior and multiplayer are unverified.

Permissions
Re-uploading or including this mod in a public mod pack requires the author's permission.
